HiveMind for your computer

You have HiveMind on your phone. Install it on your computer too: the computer runs bigger AI models, and your phone helps it. Your chats and documents never leave your devices.

How to install, step by step

Download

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 8 GB of memory (RAM) or more is best.

Download for Windows Zip file How to install on Windows

Linux

64-bit (x86-64) with a desktop: Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, Debian 12, Fedora, Linux Mint 21 or newer.

Download for Linux tar.gz file How to install on Linux

No phone app yet? HiveMind on Google Play (Android 10 or newer).

Three steps

On the computer

Download and install

Unzip, start HiveMind and allow it on your home network. About 5 minutes.

On the phone

Show the pairing code

Devices → Show pairing code. Both on the same Wi‑Fi.

On the computer

Add the phone

Devices → Add device, pick your phone, type the code, accept on the phone. Done.

What you can do

Solo

Chat with a model on the computer. Files, pictures, saved chats.

Big Model

Phones lend their memory, so the computer runs a model too big for it alone.

Team

Every device takes part of a long document or list; one writes the answer.

Connect

Other apps use your AI through an Ollama- and OpenAI-compatible address.

Everything runs on your devices: no account, no cloud. All builds: GitHub Releases.
